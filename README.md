# Expense & Budget Visualizer

Track your daily expenses and stay on top of your budget.

## What is this?

A simple tool to help you monitor your spending. Add your expenses, categorize them, and watch your budget balance update in real-time.

## How it works

1. Enter your monthly budget (optional)
2. Add each expense with a name and amount
3. Choose a category - Food, Transport, Fun, or create your own
4. View your total balance and spending patterns

## Why use this?

- Keep track of where your money goes
- Avoid overspending with real-time balance
- See your spending habits at a glance
- No account required - everything stays on your device

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Set your monthly budget (optional)
3. Start adding your expenses
4. Watch your balance update automatically

## Features

- Add and track expenses by category
- Monthly summary cards
- Pie chart showing spending breakdown
- Dark mode for late-night budget checking
- Custom categories for unique expenses
- All data saved automatically in your browser

## Need help?

This is a simple web app - just open it in your browser. No installation needed.
